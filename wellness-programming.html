Wellness Programs & Education

I build health education and wellness programs that help organizations give people better tools to understand and care for their health.

My work combines research, program design, education, and implementation. Right now, that includes corporate wellness strategy and women's health research, with menstrual health education in development.

Work With Me

Corporate Wellness

Wellness programs work better when they fit the people they are built for.

I work with organizations to research, design, and structure wellness programs around their employees, benefits, and long term goals. That can include program strategy, incentives, health education, vendor research, implementation planning, and wellness content.

Featured Project

True Health

A three year corporate wellness program built for True Homes.

I designed True Health for True Homes, a homebuilding company with a distributed workforce across North Carolina and South Carolina.

The program included a phased three year rollout, wellness incentives, health education, clinical vendor coordination, employee challenges, and a structure for connecting participation to the company's existing health plan.

In Development

Women's Health Education

A large part of Poiema Project is focused on the gap between the health decisions women are asked to make and how much they have actually been taught about their bodies.

I research women's health, menstrual health, and hormone education with a focus on making complex information easier to understand and use.

I am currently completing the Women's Hormone Health Certification Program through the Institute for Menstrual Health as I continue developing educational resources and future curriculum.

The First Twenty Years

What are young women carrying between their first period and adulthood, and where are we failing to teach them?

I wrote The First Twenty Years to look at the health issues, misconceptions, and gaps in care affecting girls and young women between ages 10 and 30.

The paper looks at menstrual health, cycle education, hormone care, nutrition, mental health, and opportunities for better education and support.
