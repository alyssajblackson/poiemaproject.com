Work With Me

Help the women you serve understand their health better.

If you serve women and want to understand their health needs or give them useful health education, I would love to help.

That could look like building or improving a wellness program, researching a women's health topic, creating educational resources, or speaking with the women in your organization.

I can step in for the research and consulting side, or help carry an idea through to a full program or resource.

Start a Conversation

I can help if you want to...

  • Build or improve a wellness program
  • Understand the health needs of the women in your organization
  • Research a women's health topic
  • Create health education or practical resources
  • Bring women's health education into your school, workplace, ministry, or organization
  • Host a speaker or workshop for women or students

Wellness Programming

I work with organizations to understand what they already offer, what the women they serve would benefit from, and how existing benefits or resources can be used more intentionally.

Audit what already exists

I can review current wellness efforts, health benefits, employee resources, and education to understand what is already working and where there may be gaps.

Understand what people need

I can help gather employee or participant feedback to understand what people would use, where they have questions, and what would make a wellness program more useful.

Research practical options

I can research benefits, vendors, health education, and programming opportunities with attention to both usefulness and cost.

Build the plan

From research and consulting to a fuller program build, I can help turn the information into a practical plan that fits your organization.

Speaking + Workshops

I am available to speak with colleges, schools, businesses, churches, Bible studies, women's groups, nonprofits, classrooms, and events.

Speaking can be tailored to the setting. In a faith-based setting, I can include Scripture and talk about what it means to care for a body designed by God alongside the health education. In a secular setting, I can keep the conversation focused on the science and research.

Understanding the menstrual cycle and what every young woman should know

Understanding hormone changes throughout the menstrual cycle

Understanding hormonal birth control and how it works

Building a healthier lifestyle through everyday choices

Lifestyle and environmental factors that can affect women's health

Faith, the female body, and caring for what God designed

Speaking fees vary based on the format, preparation, travel, and scope. I also set aside some time for pro bono opportunities when the cause is a good fit.

Research + Women's Health Education

You may not need a full program. Maybe your organization has a question about women's health or needs research before making a decision. I can also help turn complex information into education people can use.

Research + consulting

I can research women's health topics, organize findings, identify education gaps, and help your team think through what matters for the women you serve.

Education + resources

I can help create guides, presentations, curriculum support, and other health education materials built for a specific audience.
