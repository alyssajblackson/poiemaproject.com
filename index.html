Work With Me

Helping women understand their bodies so they can make informed decisions about their health.

I'm Alyssa, the founder of Poiema Project and Rooted Rhythm. I build at the intersection of women's health, education, entrepreneurship, wellness programming, research, and marketing. This site brings together what I am building, writing, researching, and designing as I learn how to help women better understand their bodies and make informed decisions about their health.

Alyssa Blackson

Women's Health Advocate and Educator

The problem I kept seeing

Too many women are making decisions about their health without ever being taught how their bodies actually work. The information exists, but it is often scattered, confusing, and difficult to know who to trust.

So I started Poiema Project.

ποίημα

poe-ee-ma

Greek. Workmanship, masterpiece.

Poiema is the Greek word for workmanship or masterpiece. It reflects a belief at the center of this work: every woman is intentionally made by God, and her body deserves to be understood and cared for like it.

Poiema Project advocates for the health and wholeness of young women, giving them the research, resources, and representation they deserve.
