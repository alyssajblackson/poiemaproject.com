Menstrual health education

Let's make the menstrual cycle make sense.

I want more girls to understand their bodies before they're expected to make decisions about them. That's why I'm developing menstrual health classes for high school and college women, along with workshops for the schools, churches, and groups that serve them.

We start with the basics, make room for questions, and talk about how this information connects to everyday life.

Why I'm building this

There is a lot we should have learned sooner.

Most of us were taught what to do when we got our first period. We spent a lot less time learning why it happens, how our hormones change, and what our cycle might tell us about our health.

I want these classes to give girls a place to ask questions and leave with a better understanding of what is happening in their bodies. I also want the adults and organizations around them to feel more equipped to support them.

Understanding your body changes the questions you know to ask.

What we'll talk about

Start with the questions that matter.

The class can be adapted to the age and needs of your group. These are the core topics I'm developing the curriculum around.

01 / The foundation

How the cycle works

What happens during a menstrual cycle, how the phases fit together, and why your period is only one part of the picture.

02 / The science

Hormones and ovulation

An approachable explanation of estrogen, progesterone, and ovulation, including why these processes matter for overall health.

03 / The patterns

Symptoms worth noticing

What a cycle can look like, how to keep track of changes, and when pain, missing periods, or other symptoms deserve a conversation with a healthcare professional.

04 / The next step

Asking informed questions

How to talk about your health with a parent, mentor, or clinician and where to look for trustworthy information when you want to keep learning.

A few good questions

Before you reach out

Are these classes for individuals or groups?

Right now, I'm developing group classes and workshops. If you're an individual interested in attending, you can reach out and tell me where you're located. I'd love to hear what you want to learn.

Can the content be used in a school or a church?

Yes. The session can be planned around your audience and setting. Faith-based discussion can be included when requested, and school content can be tailored to the age of the students and the school's requirements.

Is there a schedule or price list yet?

I'm currently developing these offerings. Reach out with your group size, location, and preferred timing, and we can discuss what may be possible.

Does a class replace medical advice?

No. These sessions are educational and are not a substitute for individualized medical care. Participants should discuss personal symptoms and treatment questions with a qualified healthcare professional.
